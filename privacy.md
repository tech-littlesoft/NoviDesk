# NoviDesk 隐私说明 <span class="en">NoviDesk Privacy Policy</span>

> **最后更新 / Last updated:** 2026-08-30<br>
> **应用 / App:** NoviDesk（项目名 NoviDesk Station 小维桌面工作站）　**版本 / Version:** 1.6.07<br>
> **发布者 / Publisher:** Littlesoft Team

## 1. 概述 <span class="en">Overview</span>

<p class="zh">NoviDesk（由 Littlesoft Team 开发）是一款运行在 Windows 上的本地桌面效率工具。它通过屏幕角落热区与键盘快捷键，帮助你快速访问常用的文件夹与文件。本应用完全在你的设备本地运行，不会连接互联网，不会收集、上传或向外发送任何个人信息或文件内容。</p>

<p class="en">NoviDesk (developed by Littlesoft Team) is a local desktop productivity utility for Windows. It helps you quickly access frequently used folders and files through screen-corner hot zones and keyboard shortcuts. The application runs entirely on your device. It does not connect to the internet and does not collect, upload, or transmit any personal information or file contents.</p>

## 2. 我们收集的信息 <span class="en">Information We Collect</span>

<p class="zh">我们不会收集你的个人信息。本应用不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地理位置、通讯录或任何可识别个人身份的信息，也不会向任何服务器发送使用数据或遥测数据。</p>

<p class="en">We do not collect your personal information. The app requires no account registration and gathers no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any personally identifiable information. It sends no usage or telemetry data to any server.</p>

## 3. 文件与文件夹访问 <span class="en">File and Folder Access</span>

<p class="zh">为向你展示文件夹内容，本应用会在你选择的目录（默认包括桌面、文档、图片、视频、下载、开始菜单程序，以及部分即时通讯软件的接收目录）中读取文件与文件夹的元数据，例如名称、大小、创建时间与修改时间。本应用仅读取元数据用于展示，绝不读取、上传或存储任何文件的正文内容。你随时可以在设置中增删被监控的目录。</p>

<p class="en">To display folder contents, the app reads metadata—such as name, size, creation time, and modification time—of files and folders in the directories you choose (by default the Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Videos, Downloads, Start Menu Programs, and receive folders of certain messaging apps). The app reads metadata only for display and never reads, uploads, or stores the actual content of any file. You can add or remove monitored folders in settings at any time.</p>

## 4. 鼠标与键盘输入 <span class="en">Mouse and Keyboard Input</span>

<p class="zh">本应用在本地安装全局鼠标钩子与键盘钩子，用于检测屏幕角落热区（自动展开面板）与快捷键操作。这些输入仅在你设备本地进行处理，用于判断触发条件；我们不会记录、保存或上传你的鼠标移动轨迹或键盘按键内容。</p>

<p class="en">The app installs local global mouse and keyboard hooks to detect screen-corner hot zones (to auto-show the panel) and keyboard shortcuts. This input is processed only on your device to determine triggers. We do not record, store, or upload your mouse movement trails or keystrokes.</p>

## 5. 授权与激活 <span class="en">License and Activation</span>

<p class="zh">NoviDesk 的基础功能免费，部分高级功能（Pro）通过离线激活码开启。激活验证在你的设备本地通过数字签名完成，不连接任何远程服务器，也不会上传硬件信息或机器指纹。激活状态仅保存在本机。应用内"升级 Pro"按钮在你主动点击时，才会打开外部购买网页（由你自行决定是否访问），该过程不会自动上传任何数据；任何付款均由该外部服务商处理，我们不会接收或保存你的支付信息。</p>

<p class="en">NoviDesk's basic features are free; some advanced (Pro) features are unlocked with an offline activation code. Activation is verified locally on your device through a digital signature—no remote server is contacted and no hardware information or machine fingerprint is uploaded. Activation state is stored only on your device. The in-app "Upgrade to Pro" button opens an external purchase webpage only when you click it voluntarily; no data is uploaded automatically in this process. Any payment is handled by that external provider; we do not receive or store your payment details.</p>

## 6. 数据存储与安全 <span class="en">Data Storage and Security</span>

<p class="zh">本应用产生的所有数据均存储在你自己的设备本地，具体包括：应用配置（位于 <code>%AppData%\NoviDesk\config.ini</code>）、激活状态文件（<code>license.dat</code>），以及调试版本可能生成的本地日志（位于 <code>%TEMP%\NoviDesk</code>）。这些数据不会离开你的设备，也不会被传输给开发者或任何第三方。卸载应用或删除上述本地文件即可清除全部本地数据。</p>

<p class="en">All data generated by the app is stored locally on your own device, specifically: application configuration (in <code>%AppData%\NoviDesk\config.ini</code>), activation state (<code>license.dat</code>), and, in debug builds, local logs (in <code>%TEMP%\NoviDesk</code>). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app or deleting the above local files removes all local data.</p>

## 7. 第三方共享 <span class="en">Third-Party Sharing</span>

<p class="zh">我们不会将你的任何数据出售、出租或共享给任何第三方。本应用不包含任何广告、统计分析或地图类第三方 SDK。</p>

<p class="en">We do not sell, rent, or share any of your data with any third party. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or map-related third-party SDKs.</p>

## 8. 儿童隐私 <span class="en">Children's Privacy</span>

<p class="zh">本应用不面向儿童，也不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，因此不存在向儿童收集数据的情况。</p>

<p class="en">The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. As the app collects no personal information, there is no collection of data from children.</p>

## 9. 你的权利 <span class="en">Your Rights</span>

<p class="zh">由于本应用不收集或传输任何个人信息，因此没有需要远程访问、更正或删除的个人信息。你对本机所有相关数据拥有完全控制权，可随时通过卸载应用或删除本地配置/日志文件来清除。</p>

<p class="en">Because the app does not collect or transmit any personal information, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete remotely. You have full control over all related local data and may clear it at any time by uninstalling the app or deleting the local configuration/log files.</p>

<p class="en">If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository (<a href="https://github.com/tech-littlesoft/NoviDesk">github.com/tech-littlesoft/NoviDesk</a>). Material changes will be announced via in-app notice or repository notes.</p>
